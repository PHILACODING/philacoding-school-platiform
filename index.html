About Our Institution

History of the School

Siphesihle High School (registered as Siphesihle Secondary School) is a public Quintile 3 No-Fee secondary school serving Ward 4 of the Hammarsdale Circuit in Inchanga.

Staff

Led by Principal Mr. D. S. Dube alongside a dedicated staff of over 30 educators maintaining an effective student-to-teacher ratio for continuous growth.

School Governing Board

Our active School Governing Body (SGB) works in close partnership with school management, parents, and community leaders to steer growth, governance, and learner welfare.

Digital Portal

Online Assessment & Academic Portal

Teachers publish tailored test assessments daily. Learners sign in to access assigned tests, view academic results, and view class timetables.

Assessment Protocol

Access to tests requires an active 8-digit Student Number starting with 26 (e.g. 26123456). Each learner can take ONE online assessment per 24-hour cycle. Test timers lock automatically upon submission.

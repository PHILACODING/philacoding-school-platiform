About Our Institution

A school connected to its community

History of the School

Siphesihle High School (registered as Siphesihle Secondary School) is a public Quintile 3 No-Fee secondary school serving Ward 4 of the Hammarsdale Circuit in Inchanga.

Staff

Led by Principal Mr. D. S. Dube alongside a dedicated staff of over 30 educators maintaining an effective student-to-teacher ratio for continuous growth.

School Governing Board

Our active School Governing Body (SGB) works in close partnership with school management, parents, and community leaders to steer growth, governance, and learner welfare.

Academics

Learning comes first

CAPS Curriculum

Comprehensive CAPS curriculum for Grades 8 through 12, preparing students for National Senior Certificate success.

Academic Support

Structured after-school tutoring, exam revision workshops, and mentorship from experienced educators.

Online Learning

Integrated daily testing modules and interactive digital learning resources allowing learners to track understanding and continuous progress online.

Admissions

Enrollment for the Academic Year

Applications are open for Grades 8 to 12. Ensure all required documentation (Learner Birth Certificate, Latest School Report, Guardian ID) is ready before submission.

Online Application

Download Form

Digital Portal

Online Assessment & Academic Portal

Teachers publish tailored test assessments daily. Learners sign in to access assigned tests, view academic results, and view class timetables.

Assessment Protocol

Each learner can take ONE online assessment per 24-hour cycle. Test timers lock automatically upon submission.
